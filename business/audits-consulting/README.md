# Audits & consulting

**Business line in Solid:** `Audits & consulting`

## What it sells

- **Missed-lead audit** — a written report on every path an inquiry can take to reach a business and which
  ones go unanswered. Credited against a later build.
- **Paid advice** — technology and automation consulting by the hour or the engagement.

## How its money is tracked

| Money | Solid account (Schedule C template) | Business line |
|---|---|---|
| Audit fees | 4010 Sales and Service Revenue | Audits & consulting |
| Consulting fees | 4010 Sales and Service Revenue | Audits & consulting |

**When an audit fee is credited against a build,** invoice the build under its own facet at the reduced
price, and say so in the line description ("less audit credit"). The audit income stays where it was
earned, and the build shows what the client actually paid for it.

## What belongs in this folder

- `playbooks/` — how an audit is run, start to finish.
- `templates/` — the audit report skeleton with placeholders only.

Not here: any audited business's name, findings or report.

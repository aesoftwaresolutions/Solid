# Finance & admin

**Support facet — not a business line.** Accountant, insurance, registration fees and the like are left
unassigned in Solid.

## The company's own books run in Solid

The company keeps its books in its own product. Setting up the facets:

1. Open the entity's **Settings → Business lines** and add the five revenue facets exactly as named in
   [business/README.md](../README.md).
2. On every invoice, choose the business line before finalizing.
3. When categorizing a bank transaction that belongs to one facet, choose its business line in the review
   queue. Leave shared costs unassigned.
4. Each month, open **Reports → By business line** for the month. *Shared / overhead* should hold only
   costs that truly serve every facet; anything else there is a categorizing miss to fix.

Business lines are for management reporting. They do not change the tax-line report or anything a preparer
receives — Schedule C is still filed for the whole business.

## What belongs in this folder

- `month-end.md` — the month-end routine above, expanded as it settles.
- `calendar.md` — recurring filing and renewal dates (no account numbers).

Not here: bank statements, tax returns, EIN letters or any other document — those go in Solid's encrypted
document vault.

# Software products

**Business line in Solid:** `Software products`

## What it sells

- **Solid** — this repository: self-hosted accounting and US tax preparation support, sold as a
  purchase-gated licence with a desktop installer and a server install.
- **Productized assets** — ready-made automation bundles, setup kits and prompt packs, built once and sold
  repeatedly.

## How its money is tracked

| Money | Solid account (Schedule C template) | Business line |
|---|---|---|
| Licence and product sales | 4010 Sales and Service Revenue | Software products |
| Marketplace and payment-processor fees on those sales | 6230 Bank and Merchant Fees | Software products |
| Code-signing certificates, app-store or marketplace accounts | 6220 Software and Subscriptions | Software products |

## What belongs in this folder

- `roadmap.md` — product-level priorities that sit above Solid's own [roadmap](../../docs/design/07-roadmap.md).
- `catalog/` — public descriptions of each productized asset.

Not here: customer lists, licence keys or sales figures.

# Sales & marketing

**Support facet — not a business line.** Its costs are left unassigned in Solid unless a campaign exists for
a single facet (a paid ad for website rebuilds only goes to `Websites & hosting`).

## What it covers

- The company website and its pricing section.
- Finding prospects, the outreach sequence and phone follow-up.
- Intake: the first call, the checklist, and the proposal.

## Where things live

| Material | Where |
|---|---|
| Public positioning, service descriptions | Here, and on the company website |
| Prospect lists, contact details, outreach copy, suppression list | **Private workspace only — never this repo** |
| Proposals and signed agreements | Private workspace only |

## What belongs in this folder

- `positioning.md` — who the company serves and why, in public language.
- `playbooks/` — the general shape of intake and follow-up, with no names.

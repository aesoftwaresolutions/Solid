# Websites & hosting

**Business line in Solid:** `Websites & hosting`

## What it sells

- **Website design and rebuilds** — WordPress sites, typically five pages with online booking.
- **Hosting and site care** — sites hosted on the company VPS, kept updated, backed up and monitored.
- **Google Business Profile upkeep** — when bundled with a rebuild.

## How its money is tracked

| Money | Solid account (Schedule C template) | Business line |
|---|---|---|
| Design and build fees | 4010 Sales and Service Revenue | Websites & hosting |
| Hosting and care plans | 4010 Sales and Service Revenue (a repeating invoice) | Websites & hosting |
| Premium themes, plugins or a server bought for one client | 5010 Purchases and Materials | Websites & hosting |
| The shared VPS, domains for the company's own site | 6220 Software and Subscriptions | *unassigned* |

## What belongs in this folder

- `playbooks/` — site launch checklist, monthly care routine, restore procedure.
- `starter-theme/` — reusable theme components with no client branding.

Not here: client logos, copy, hosting credentials or analytics exports.
